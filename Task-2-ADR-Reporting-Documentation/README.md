Task 2 – ADR Reporting & Documentation

1. Introduction

ADR reporting is an important part of pharmacovigilance. It involves systematic collection, documentation and reporting of suspected adverse drug reactions.

2. Objective

- To understand ADR reporting procedures.
- To learn structured ADR documentation.
- To perform causality assessment.
- To understand the importance of patient safety.

3. Case Scenario

A fictional patient developed skin rash, itching and redness after taking Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid.

4. ADR Assessment

Suspected Drug: Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid (Augmentin).

Suspected ADR: Skin rash, itching and redness.

Severity: Moderate.

WHO-UMC Causality: Probable (training assessment).

5. Documentation

A structured ADR reporting form was prepared containing patient details, suspected drug information, reaction details, treatment, outcome and causality assessment.

6. Conclusion

ADR reporting helps in monitoring medicine safety, identifying suspected adverse reactions and promoting patient safety.

Note: This is a fictional educational case, not an actual patient report.
